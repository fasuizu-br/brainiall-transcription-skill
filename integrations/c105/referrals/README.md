# C105 referral and affiliate blueprint

Offer a corporate partner a single attributed link to an authorized transcript
trial. Do not buy a list, preassign commission, or treat a share/reply as an
independent buyer. A commission is payable only after the underlying Stripe
payment, provider settlement, and reconciliation are verified.

## Gate

PartnerStack and Impact-style marketplaces require application/approval. Keep
the opt-in page public, but do not claim enrollment or partner status without a
receipt.

References: <https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009185034-What-is-the-Marketplace>
and <https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/working-with-brands/applying-to-brands/find-and-apply-to-brands>.
