# C107 creator caption QA

This recipe targets a creator-owned YouTube video. It produces transcript,
SRT and WebVTT for human review, then lets the owner upload captions through
their own YouTube Studio account.

- Require written authorization or Subtitle Editor access.
- Use a local file or an owner-provided export; never scrape channels.
- Compare timing, speaker labels and readability before delivery.
- Do not claim views, ranking, channel access or payment from an upload.
- Link any offer to the Brainiall checkout and count revenue only after Stripe
  settlement and independent reconciliation.

The platform workflow is documented in [YouTube's caption guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734796?hl=en-EN) and [Subtitle Editor access](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11983512?hl=en).
