# Medium publication

Propose a factual workflow story to a publication whose readers already do
podcast, education, or accessibility work. Follow the publication's own
submission rules and disclose the product's limits. A published story or read
count is not first value; the CTA must lead to an owned, attributable test.

- [Publish a first story](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/225168768-Writing-and-publishing-your-first-story)
- [Submit to a publication](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/213904978-How-to-submit-a-story-to-a-publication)

Do not claim acceptance, distribution, or sales without a receipt.
