# LinkedIn Page and newsletter

Use a company Page or newsletter only for a concrete job: turning an
interview, webinar, or lesson into a reviewable transcript and SRT. The
publisher must use a corporate account and obtain editorial approval. Link to
the owned Transcreve route with a unique `c106|linkedin-*` intent; never
automate personal profiles or scrape subscribers.

Evidence and rules: [newsletter access criteria](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a596269/)
and [Page newsletters](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a596833).
Reads and subscribers are discovery signals only. Count a buyer only after the
server-side event chain reaches Stripe, payout, and bank reconciliation.
