# Reddit and Indie Hackers

Start by observing the job language: caption timing, speaker labels, or a
repeatable transcript handoff. Respond only when the local rules permit it,
with one useful answer and no mass link drops. A moderator or community owner
can opt in to a small fixture pilot; no purchased list, crawler, autoresponder,
or duplicate contact is allowed.

- [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- [Indie Hackers terms](https://www.indiehackers.com/terms)

Use synthetic or explicitly authorized media. A comment, click, or referral is
not revenue; preserve a deduped intent and reconcile any Stripe payment.
